📜 Driver Partner Code of Conduct & Rules

Welcome to the BizHub Fleet network! To maintain data tracking accuracy, preserve secure payment transactions, and protect your commercial dispatcher privileges across Ghana, all driver partners must strictly follow these rules:

•
Maintain Accurate Availability: Only set your status to Online if you are genuinely ready to mount motorcycle/vehicle transits immediately.
•
Prompt Status Tracking: Update your trip progress bar inputs instantly (Arrived at Pickup ➔ Mark Delivered) to keep the ordering vendor's dashboard synchronized.
•
Fair Pricing & Integrity: Honor the delivery markdown values agreed upon during your direct merchant WhatsApp arrangement loops. Misleading or fraudulent overcharging results in immediate profile suspension.
•
Zero Tolerance for Conduct Violations: Any abusive behavior or failed delivery disputes reported by verified clients will route directly to the Admin Center for immediate enforcement actions.
