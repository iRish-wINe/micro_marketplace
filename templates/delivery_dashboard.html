What registering as a Delivery Service means

Registering as a delivery service company or independent delivery agent on BizHub means you are joining the marketplace as a provider that can receive delivery requests from participating vendors and Fast Food businesses.

What you are expected to do:
  • Keep your service name, phone number, operating location, service area, delivery type and logo accurate and up to date.
  • Set yourself to Available only when you are genuinely ready to accept delivery work; use Unavailable when you are not accepting requests.
  • Review requests carefully, then accept or decline them based on your actual capacity and ability to complete the delivery.
  • When a request is accepted, use the dashboard to keep its status accurate through pickup and delivery.
  • Communicate responsibly and follow BizHub rules. Do not use the platform for unsafe, fraudulent, abusive or misleading activity.

Verification: You may request a BizHub verification review. Verification is a trust review by BizHub and is not a guarantee of delivery requests, income, payment, customer satisfaction or business performance. Keep your account information truthful so BizHub can review your service fairly.
